   file: sign of d(z/|a|)/dt      user: sign of dz/dt.

   band so that "sitting still" is not counted either way.

== 3. WHAT EACH RULE READS WHEN THE SHIPPED GATE FIRES ==
   The gate acts on the file's statistic. On exactly those frames, what does
   the user's rule say the oar was doing? 100% = the two rules never part.

== 5. HOW BIG IS THE DISAGREEMENT, AGAINST THE BARS? ==
   A disagreement only matters if it can reach a bar. Both bars are >= 4.0, so
   any frame whose |_w_fa| is under 4.0 is a frame the game never looks at.

   capture                   disagree    max |_w_fa| min |a|/g there
   left_strokes.txt                38           0.49           0.80
   air_swings.txt                  27           2.46           0.54
   fake_capture.txt                17           0.67           0.87
   rev_real_rev1.txt               11           3.20           0.65
   rev_real_rev2.txt                8           2.28           0.57
   rev_real_rev3.txt               10           2.09           0.95
   rev_real_rev4.txt                3           1.68           1.06

   the largest disagreement anywhere is |_w_fa| = 3.20, against bars of 4.0 and 5.0.
   nothing below 4.0 can fire, so the division cannot change a decision.

== 7. THE OTHER WAY A SIGN CAN FEEL WRONG: LAG AT THE TURNAROUND ==
   _w_fa is an ema, so after you reverse the oar it keeps reading the old
   direction for a few frames. This is how long, on the frames either side of
   a real turnaround, and how big the stale reading is while it lasts.

   capture                     turns   lag med   lag max stale |_w_fa|
   left_strokes.txt                5       1.0         7          0.99
   air_swings.txt                  6       2.5         5          4.00
   rev_real_rev1.txt               4       0.5         1          2.13
   rev_real_rev2.txt               2       3.0         3          2.29
   rev_real_rev3.txt               1       4.0         4          1.28
   rev_real_rev4.txt               2       1.5         2          1.47

   one frame is 10 ms. A stroke is a few hundred ms, so this is one to two
   frames of the wrong sign at each end, while the rate is smallest.
